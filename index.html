Brand guide for the collector’s app. Open your vault.

A vault for people
who collect the beast.

BeastVault is an independent app for collecting Monster Energy cans: every flavour, limited edition, and collab. Collectors keep, protect, and brag about their finds, and a vault is the perfect picture of that. The logo shows it directly: split vault doors opening on a can silhouette, with neon cut marks flagging what’s rare.

Positioning

For Monster collectors who hunt limited editions, trade stories, and want a proper home for their shelf. BeastVault is the tracker that treats every can like a trophy.

Personality

Raw, confident, a little feral. Direct, never loud for the sake of it. We mean what we say and keep the claims short.

Core idea

“Unlock the beast.” Every can you find is kept, tracked, and shown off. The app is the lock that protects your collection and the key that opens it.

Logo

One mark, two parts: the vault-door emblem and the BEASTVAULT wordmark below it. The two doors are the monogram, the gap between them holds a can silhouette, and the slashes cut across both.

Primary

Full colour on Vault Black. This is the home of the logo. On lime, use the one-colour black version.

Clear space

Keep one door-width of empty space on every side. That’s about a quarter of the emblem’s height.

Minimum size

Emblem alone: 24 px or 8 mm. Emblem with wordmark: 96 px or 28 mm. Below that, use the emblem only.

Do

  • use the supplied files, scaled proportionally
  • place it on black, Deep Vault, or flat lime
  • let the claws overlap photos at the edge of a layout

Don’t

  • recolour the slashes outside the palette
  • stretch, tilt, or add glows and outlines
  • place it on busy photography without a black panel

Colour

All five colours come straight from the logo. Black does most of the work, lime carries type, and the two neons are for the slashes and the single thing you want seen.

Typography

The wordmark is hand-cut and rough, so the system pairs it with something tall and hard, then something quiet for reading.

Crack it open

Anton for headlines, product names, and numbers. Tight leading, never lighter than regular.

Unlock the beast

Green Energy carries the charge of the wordmark. Use it for taglines, rarity labels, and short call-outs, one line at a time.

Barlow carries all body copy, labels, and legal text. Regular for reading, 600 for emphasis.

Scale: 64 / 40 / 24 / 17 / 14. Sentence case for everything except flavour names on cans.

Voice

Short sentences. Active verbs. We talk like a fellow collector: excited about the hunt, honest about what’s in your vault.

Unlock the beast.

Primary tagline. Store listing, onboarding, the one-line sign-off.

Earn the open.

Achievements, streaks, and rare-find unlocks. Pairs with the vault door motif.

Every can, kept.

Collection screens, empty states, and the store listing.

We sound like

  • “New can added. Your vault just got heavier.”
  • “Rare find. Open the doors and show it off.”
  • “Shelf empty? Scan your first can to start.”

We never sound like

  • imply the app is made or run by Monster
  • alter, recolour, or merge Monster’s logo with ours
  • shame people for a small collection

Graphic language

The slash

Cut marks in Beast Green and Voltage Yellow that flag rare finds, milestones, and streaks. Redraw them as our own shape (see below). Never more than one set per view.

The doors

Layouts split down the middle: content behind two Deep Vault panels. In motion, doors open once, left and right, then stay open.

The can gap

The narrow black slot with the can silhouette is the brand’s hero detail. Use it as a masked reveal whenever a new can is added.

Imagery: low-key, hard side light, deep blacks, one lime accent in frame. Real collections on real shelves, plus dark-mode app screens.

Staying independent

BeastVault is an independent app built around Monster, and it uses Monster’s official logo with permission. The logo appears only as supplied, unaltered, and kept apart from the BeastVault mark: never merged into it, recoloured, or used as our app name. Our own cut marks stay a distinct shape so the two brands never blur. Written permission and Monster’s usage guidelines are kept on file, and the store listing and About screen state the relationship in the wording Monster approves, for example: “BeastVault is an independent app that uses the Monster Energy logo with permission. Monster Energy is a trademark of its owner.”
